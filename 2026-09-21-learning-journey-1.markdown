### GROUP PRESENTATION - Computers in Space

## Introduction
This post is a reflection of my first group presentation in the  course craft of computing. By this experience, I learned many important things about teamwork, communication, and presentation skills.I done my first presentation on my own was graet experience for me.

## Preparation Process
Before the presentation, our team discussed the topic and divided the work among ourselves. Each member was given a specific part to prepare. We also practiced together to make sure the presentation flowed smoothly.

# Our  Topic
Our presentation topic was Computers in Space.
We discussed how computers are used in satellites, spacecraft, and space missions.
Computers help in navigation, communication, data collection, and controlling systems in space.

# My Part
I handled the summary part of the presentation.
I summarized all the important points covered by my teammates (introduction, evolution, uses, and challenges of computers in space).
I also included key takeaways and future expectations related to computers in space.
My role was to conclude the presentation in a clear and organized way.
I made sure the summary connected all the parts presented by the team.

## Teamwork and Collaboration
Working in a group taught me the importance of coordination. We shared ideas, helped each other, and made sure everyone was clear about their responsibilities. This experience improved my ability to work with others.
